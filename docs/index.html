Create Your Account

This is your personal login. All devices that sign in with the same email and password will synchronize data automatically.

1Sign In Anywhere

Use your email and password to log in on any device. Your data follows you.

2Sync Across Devices

Install the app on multiple phones or tablets. All devices with the same email share rooms, bookings, guests, and invoices.

3Role-Based Access

After first login, assign staff roles (Admin, Manager, Receptionist, Housekeeper, Restaurant, Kitchen). Restaurant takes orders and payment; Kitchen sees the same live order queue (prep / ready / served) without billing.
